About Kaboma

A living journey through Kenya.

Kaboma Cultural Dancers is a Kenyan cultural and heritage dance group dedicated to preserving and sharing Kenya's traditions through live dance, music, storytelling, traditional attire and audience participation.

Our performances bring together cultural expressions from different Kenyan communities and present them as one professionally coordinated experience.

From corporate events and conferences to hotels, weddings, tourism experiences, festivals, expos and VIP welcomes, Kaboma creates cultural moments that guests can see, hear, understand and participate in.

Inquire About a Performance
Heritage in Motion

What truly makes Kaboma different is this:

A Kaboma performance is designed as an experience — not simply a sequence of dances.

01 — THE WHOLE OF KENYA

The Whole of Kenya in One Show

Kenya has more than 40 tribes and communities, each with unique languages, traditions, attire, instruments, rhythms and dances. Kaboma brings these cultural expressions together in one performance, taking audiences on a journey across Kenya.

02 — THE STORY

We Tell the Story Behind Every Dance

Our live cultural narrator helps audiences understand what they are seeing — including the community being represented, the meaning of movements, songs, instruments and traditions behind each cultural presentation.

03 — PARTICIPATION

Interactive Cultural Experience

Guests are invited to participate and dance with the performers. This turns the presentation into a shared cultural moment where people can experience Kenya through movement, rhythm and interaction.

The Full Kaboma Experience

More than a performance.

The full experience can bring together dancers, live percussionists and a cultural narrator to create a complete Kenyan heritage presentation.

  • Traditional Kenyan dance and movement
  • Live drumming and percussion
  • Cultural storytelling and narration
  • Traditional attire and visual presentation
  • Audience participation and interaction
  • Adaptable formats for different event programs
